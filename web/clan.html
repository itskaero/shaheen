The Spirit of Shaheen

Allama Muhammad Iqbal used the image of the Shaheen to awaken a spirit in the youth — a spirit of freedom, courage, ambition, self-reliance and endless striving for higher goals.

نہیں تیرا نشیمن قصرِ سلطانی کے گنبد پر
تو شاہیں ہے بسیرا کر پہاڑوں کی چٹانوں میں

Your dwelling is not upon the dome of the royal palace; you are a Shaheen — make your home upon the mountain rocks.

— Allama Iqbal, Bal-e-Jibril

تو شاہیں ہے، پرواز ہے کام تیرا
ترے سامنے آسماں اور بھی ہیں

You are a Shaheen; flight is your purpose — there are yet more skies before you.

— Allama Iqbal

The Shaheen doesn't settle. It doesn't fear the heights. It keeps flying, keeps fighting, and never stops chasing bigger skies. That is the mindset of Shaheen.
