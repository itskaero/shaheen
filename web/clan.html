The Spirit of Shaheen

Where the name comes from

A Bird, and an Instruction

Iqbal never wrote the shaheen as an ornament. He wrote it as a standard to be held to — a bird defined by where it is willing to go next, not by where it already sits.

تو شاہیں ہے، پرواز ہے کام تیرا
ترے سامنے آسماں اور بھی ہیں You are a Shaheen — flight is your work. There are yet more skies before you. Allama Iqbal
نہیں تیرا نشیمن قصرِ سلطانی کے گنبد پر
تو شاہیں ہے بسیرا کر پہاڑوں کی چٹانوں میں Your dwelling is not upon the dome of the royal palace; you are a Shaheen — make your home upon the mountain rocks. Allama Iqbal · Bal-e-Jibril
