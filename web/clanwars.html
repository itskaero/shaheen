How Clan Wars works

  1. Be on a teamJoin a team on BRAWLISTAN. Captains add players with /team, or the roster follows your in-game clan.
  2. Play rankedEvery ranked 1v1 win is 10 points, and every rating point you gain this week adds one more. Losses cost nothing.
  3. Best five countYour team's score is its five best members this week, so a big roster can't win on numbers alone.
  4. Win the weekThe war ends Monday 00:00 PKT. Weekly wins build the season table. Check yours anytime with /clanwars.
