How coaching works

  1. Pick a coach who's Taking students.
  2. In the BRAWLISTAN Discord, run /coach request, choose the coach and say what you want help with.
  3. The coach accepts in the coaching channel, and you sort out a time together.

Coaches are members staff gave the Coach role. Coaching on BRAWLISTAN is free and community-run.
